🎛️ Everything it does

Small app. Serious memory.

Powerful where it counts, invisible the rest of the time.

♾️

Always-on rolling buffer

A continuous loop quietly captures everything, keeping the last 100 hours on your device (space permitting) and last 500 hours in the cloud, so the moment is already saved before you knew you wanted it.

☁️

Automatic cloud backup

Encrypted on your phone, then synced to the cloud over Wi-Fi. Lose your phone, keep your memories. Only smart-uploads when you're on power & network.

🔖

One-tap bookmarks

Heard something good? Tap once — or say the word — and Sonus Auris flags that slice of the timeline so it's trivial to find later.

✂️

Clean clip export

Trim any moment to a tidy clip for your DAW, notes, or private review. You decide if and where it is shared. Lossless audio, no re-encoding mush.

🔋

Built to sip battery

Efficient on-device encoding and a battery-aware uploader mean it runs all day without turning your phone into a hand-warmer.

🔑

Keys you control

Recordings are encrypted with keys derived on your device. Not even we can listen. Privacy isn't a setting — it's the architecture.

🎚️

“Day of My Life” on SoundCloud

Opt in and each day quietly becomes a private 24-hour “Day of My Life” track on your SoundCloud — captioned with on-device AI notes like “Band practice” or “Drive”. It keeps a rolling last 100 days and prunes the rest. Private by default, only when you ask.

🎵

Your Spotify memories

Link Spotify and the songs Sonus Auris recognises roll into a private “Sonus Auris Memories” playlist — automatically de-duplicated, so every track you actually lived through shows up once. Opt-in.

🆔

Knows your voice

A quick one-time recording teaches Sonus Auris your voiceprint. After that it can tell your voice from everyone else's — so insights are about you, and your words stay yours.

🌙

Sleep & wellness signals

Listening overnight too, it flags patterns worth a closer look — snoring, breathing pauses that may point to sleep apnea, a cough that lingers. Insights, not diagnoses; bring them to your doctor.

🗣️

Speech & diction coach

FFT-powered frequency analysis of your own speech catches mumbled words, mispronunciations, filler-word habits, and clumsy phrasing — then nudges you toward clearer diction and tighter grammar over time.

🔒 Privacy is the whole point

An always-on recorder you can actually trust

An app that listens all day only makes sense if it's locked down all day. Sonus Auris is built privacy-first: encrypted on your device, stored as ciphertext we can't read, and never sold, mined, or used to train anything. Core recording and available audio analysis run on-device. Features that use an external recognition service are separately disclosed and opt-in; encrypted backup never makes the service a silent listener. You hold the recording keys — full stop.

🔐
Encrypted on-deviceAudio is encrypted the instant it's captured, using keys derived on your phone.
🙈
Zero-knowledge cloudYour backups are ciphertext to us. We store bytes we can't read — by design.
🚫
No ads, no resaleWe don't sell, mine, or train on your audio. Ever. The app is the product, not you.
🗑️
Yours to deleteWipe a clip, a day, or everything — locally and in the cloud — whenever you want.

🎧 Record responsibly. Recording and evidence laws vary by place, and whether any recording is admissible is always up to the court. Sonus Auris gives you tamper-evident clip metadata, capture times, optional location tags, and the controls to manage them — it does not certify authenticity or admissibility. You stay in charge of when and where it listens.
