Delete your Sonus Auris account and data

You can delete your account directly in the app: open Settings → Delete account. This confirms with you, deletes your account and Sonus Auris backend data (including encrypted backups), and wipes app-private on-device recordings, keys, and tokens.

To request deletion without the app, emailalexander.d.mills@gmail.comfrom the address associated with your account (or include the device/account ID shown in the app's Settings) with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and complete deletion, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted vs. retained

DataOn deletion
Account record, Supabase Auth user, auth/device tokensDeleted
Clip metadata and encrypted backups held by Sonus AurisDeleted
App-private on-device recordings, keys, tokensWiped by in-app deletion; a web/email request cannot remotely erase a device
Local copies you deliberately exported through the system share sheet or a user-selected desktop destinationOutside Sonus Auris app storage and automatic retention; you control these copies and must delete them from the destination you selected
Clips you backed up to your own storage (S3/Drive/OneDrive/iCloud)You control these; delete them in that service
Minimal transaction logs required by lawRetained only as long as legally required, then deleted

Exporting a local copy does not delay deletion of the app-private recording and does not make the exported destination part of your Sonus Auris account.

Target: complete deletion within 30 days of a verified request.
