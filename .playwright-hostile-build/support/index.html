Sonus Auris support

Need help with recording, your account, or a connected cloud destination? Sonus Auris is open source, and support requests are tracked publicly so fixes and workarounds remain searchable.

Get help

  • Open a support request on GitHub.
  • Review the source and current issues.
  • Read the privacy policy.
  • Delete your account and data.

Before opening a request

Include your phone model, OS version, app version, and the exact step that failed. Never post passwords, Supabase tokens, cloud access keys, private recording links, or audio you do not intend to make public.

Urgent privacy requests

Use the account-deletion pathway above. The monitored privacy email will be published on that page before the store release.
